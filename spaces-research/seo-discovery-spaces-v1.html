1. Bottom line

  1. Demand clusters around jobs, not around tools. The repeated questions are: per-app mouse and key remaps, text expansion that works on both Windows and Mac, a global mic mute, showing keystrokes on screen, one-handed and sticky-modifier input, and getting the same Ctrl/Cmd shortcuts on both OSes. Each one has a SERP full of listicles and forum threads, and almost none of those answers mention a tool that scripts the job on both Windows and macOS. That is the honest gap. (Inference from SERP shape.)
  2. "Alternative" SERPs are owned by directories and competitor blogs. AlternativeTo, Slashdot, SoftwareSuggest, and vendor blogs from TextExpander and Text Blaze hold the AutoHotkey, Keyboard Maestro and Karabiner "alternative" results (Observed in SERP). Rebind should only enter them with use-both positioning, mainly through directory listings. It should not publish "X replacement" pages.
  3. There is a brand-accuracy problem right now. Two third-party pages that surface for "Rebind rebind.gg" say Rebind runs on Linux (freedom251.com, techyorker.com, both dated Sep 2026). Locked product truth says Linux is unsupported. On top of that, AlternativeTo's only "Rebind" listing is a different product, an open-source Mac app by Blinks44 (Verified). These are the first fixes, before any discovery push. They are pure answer-accuracy work and need no promotion.
  4. Rebind's indexable footprint is small and lopsided. The live sitemap holds 83 URLs: 56 marketplace script pages, 11 /chat/ pages, 4 /compare/ pages, 2 product pages and 7 top-level pages. It has no docs, no recipe or guide pages, and no /r/ pages (Verified). The job-cluster queries above have no Rebind destination to rank.
  5. Mission 001 pages are not SEO targets. They are private test editions marked noindex, nofollow (Verified in mission1/*-archive.html and the reading-edition footer, "Private test edition · v2 · noindex"). They are absent from the sitemap. Nothing in this report recommends publishing or indexing them.
  6. Recommended order for the CEO: (a) fix the Linux misstatements and the name collision (owner: Dean approval, plus a factual correction request to the two sites); (b) build 6–8 indexable recipe pages for the job clusters on rebind.gg; (c) only then list Rebind in directories, so the link equity lands somewhere useful. Every external step needs Dean's approval.
