7. Positioning note — mobile-in-development and the multitool narrative

[Inference]

Rebind’s present promise is a programmable input layer on the computer you already use, alongside tools you already have: “Command, don’t configure,” use-both rather than replace. The install-ready offer remains the free desktop app on Windows 10/11 64-bit and macOS 13+ Apple Silicon. That is still the supported list, the letter CTA, and the education bet in Full Market Research.

“Android and iOS apps are in development” can sit beside that story as an optional, secondary fact: the layer may reach more devices later. It is not a present capability, not a reason to install today, and not a “cross-device” or “on your phone” promise. The multitool idea does not currently extend to a phone running scripts or macros. Until product truth exists for what the mobile apps will actually do, the desktop job is the only honest headline and the only honest CTA. Mention mobile after the supported-today list, never instead of it, and never as the hook.

This inference does not add features, dates, or a role for Link or BYOH on mobile.
